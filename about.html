ZION TODAY

More than a place. A people.

Zion Church is a community of believers brought together by our love for Jesus Christ, His Word and His people.

We believe that church is not simply a building we enter each week. It is a family where people can encounter God, discover purpose, grow in faith and walk alongside one another.
